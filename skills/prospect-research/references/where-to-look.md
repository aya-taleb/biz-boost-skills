# Where to look

| what you want | B2B company | local business or office |
| --- | --- | --- |
| what they do, size | their site (about, team, careers), public filings if listed | their site, map listing as a page you read, building directory, the user's own visit notes |
| what changed | press page, blog, local business journal, industry news | local news, permit or license search, "now open" posts, new listing |
| priorities | job posts (roles, tools named, "you will fix..."), founder talks or posts about work | job posts, menu or service changes, reviews that repeat a complaint |
| who decides | team page, press quotes, conference speaker pages | owner named on their site or in local press; else "office manager" or "front desk" |
| tools they use | job posts, integration or partner pages, case studies on vendor sites | the booking or ordering system their site links to |

## What not to use

- Personal social accounts, family details, home address, personal phone.
- Reviews or comments as a way to find a person. A review tells you about the company.
- Anything behind a login, or anything a site's terms forbid collecting.
- Paid data sources without the user's yes on the cost.
