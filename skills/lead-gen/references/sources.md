# Where to look, and what is fair to take

## Intent signals (for the profile and for the "signals" column)

| signal | where | what to take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People asking for an alternative | Reddit, Hacker News, niche forums, Slack or Discord communities the user is in | the problem, the segment, the competitor named. Not the poster |
| Job posts | the company's careers page, public job boards | the company, the role, the tool or problem the post names, the date |
| Review-site categories | G2 or Capterra category and comparison pages | which segments buy, which competitors they compare. Not the reviewers |
| Funding, new office, new location | company news page, local business journal, press releases | company and date |
| Local openings and moves | city permit search, chamber of commerce news, local news, "now open" signs the user saw | company, address, date |

## Lead sources by business type

| business | good places |
| --- | --- |
| B2B app or service | the user's own customer list, conference sponsor and exhibitor pages, industry association member directories that are public, partner directories (for example an app marketplace's listed partners), company sites |
| Local service to businesses | the user's own walk past (they note names), building tenant directories in lobbies, chamber of commerce member lists, coworking spaces' public member pages, business improvement district maps |
| Local service to people | not a lead list job. Use local-outreach for events, partners and walk-ins |

## What is fair

- Company facts on the company's own site: fine, link them.
- A name or email the company publishes for business contact: fine, note the page.
- A name from a review, comment or forum post: not for outreach.
- Personal social profiles, home addresses, personal phones: never.
- Bulk downloads from a site that forbids them: never, even if a tool can do it.
- A connector or export the user pays for: fine, under that provider's terms.
