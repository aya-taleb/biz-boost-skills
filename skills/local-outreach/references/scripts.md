# Worked examples

These show the shape. Swap in the user's real offer, area and proof from founder-context.
Words in {{braces}} are things only the user knows.

## Lunch subscription reaching offices

Front desk:
> Hi, I'm {{name}} from {{business}}. We bring street-food lunches from local food trucks to
> office lobbies. Who looks after team lunches or office perks here?

To the office manager, in person:
> We deliver lunch from {{number}} local trucks to your lobby at {{time}}. People pick 3 or 5
> days a week. You don't have to organize anything. Could I bring lunch for 10 of your team one
> day next week, so they can try it?

If they already have a caterer:
> Makes sense. We're not trying to replace the team lunch. This is for the other days, when
> people buy their own. Want me to leave a menu for the break room?

Follow-up email after a visit, subject "lunch tasting for {{company}}":
> Hi {{name}}, thanks for your time at the front desk today. As promised, here's the short
> version: {{one-line offer}}. Could I bring lunch for 10 on {{day, date}}? I'll handle setup and
> cleanup. {{sender}}, {{business}}, {{street address}}. Not for you? Reply "no" and I won't
> email again.

Building manager:
> We'd like to offer your tenants a lunch option from local trucks, delivered to the lobby,
> with no cost to the building. We'd need {{a delivery spot / a 2-hour lobby table on
> Wednesdays}}. We clean up after every drop. Could we try it for {{2 weeks}}?

Coworking community manager:
> Your members could get lunch delivered without leaving the space. Would you like to try a
> member tasting on a Friday? I'd bring it, you'd just mention it in the member chat.

## Cleaning or home service reaching small offices

Front desk:
> Hi, I'm {{name}}, I run {{business}}, a cleaning company here in {{area}}. Who handles the
> office cleaning contract?
To the decider:
> Can I give you a free quote for a walk-through? Takes 15 minutes, and I can do it today.

## Gym or studio reaching nearby workplaces

To the people team:
> We're two blocks away. Would your team like a free lunchtime class for up to {{number}}
> people? If they like it, we have a workplace rate.

## Phone, when you can't walk in

> Hi, this is {{name}} from {{business}}. Who would decide on team lunches or office perks
> there? ... Great, what's the best way to send them a short note?
