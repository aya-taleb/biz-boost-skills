# Terms to check, by business type

A reminder list of decisions, not legal wording.

| business | terms worth deciding before you send |
| --- | --- |
| Software subscription | billing period, seats and how to add or remove them, cancel notice, data export when they leave, price lock for how long, setup or import fee |
| Service or project | deposit, what counts as done, number of revision rounds, change requests, who owns the work, late payment |
| Food or catering for offices | order cutoff time, minimum order, delivery window, what happens with no-shows or leftovers, dietary labels and allergen disclaimer, price per head and how headcount changes are handled |
| Retainer | hours or scope per month, rollover or not, notice to end, response times |
| Local job (repair, cleaning, install) | site access, materials included or not, warranty, weather or reschedule rules, payment on completion |
