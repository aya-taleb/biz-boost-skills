# Channels by business type

Fit is a starting guess. The user's own results beat this table after 3 weeks.

| channel | B2B app or service | local service to offices or shops | local service to people | notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm intros and referrals | strong | strong | strong | always first if the user has a network (referral-asks) |
| Personal cold email | strong when the list is good | support | weak | needs research per lead; CAN-SPAM rules (lead-email) |
| LinkedIn notes and DMs | strong for roles active there | support for office managers and HR | weak | user sends by hand, daily invite limits (linkedin-dm) |
| Phone | medium for small businesses | medium | weak | short, polite, ask for the right person |
| Walk-ins | weak | strong | medium | bring something to leave or taste (local-outreach) |
| Events and meetups | medium | strong if local | strong | pick 1 or 2 a month and follow up within 2 days |
| Partners (non-competing businesses that serve the same buyer) | strong | strong | strong | a building manager, a coworking space, a bookkeeper for small firms |
| Communities (forums, Slack groups, Reddit) | medium | weak | medium | show up as yourself, follow the rules, help first, no mass posting |
| Content and social | slow | slow | medium | belongs in the marketing plan, not here |
| Paid ads | test only with a budget | test only | test only | not in this skill; costs money, needs the user's yes |
