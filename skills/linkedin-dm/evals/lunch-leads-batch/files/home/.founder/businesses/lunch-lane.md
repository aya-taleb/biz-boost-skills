# Lunch Lane

- slug: lunch-lane
- pitch: A weekly street food lunch subscription for office teams in Seattle. Rotating local food trucks, delivered to the lobby at 12:15.
- who it serves (ICP): office managers and people-ops leads at 20 to 200 person companies in South Lake Union and Downtown Seattle.
- stage: early revenue, 6 office customers, pilot started August 2026.
- goals (next 90 days): 15 paying offices; 2 new food truck partners; one property manager partnership.
- topics: office lunch and team rituals; local food trucks and the people who run them; running a small food logistics business; what office managers deal with.
- tone: warm, direct, local. Words to avoid: foodie, yummy, synergy, hack, cheap. <!-- allow-words -->
- proof the founder can use:
  - 6 offices on weekly plans since August 2026.
  - 9 food trucks in the rotation.
  - Average lobby drop time 12:15, late twice in 11 weeks.
- off-limits: naming customer companies without their OK; health claims about food; prices of individual trucks.
