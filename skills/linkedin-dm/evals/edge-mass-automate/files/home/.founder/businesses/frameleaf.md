# Frameleaf

- slug: frameleaf
- pitch: An app that turns a short written story into a 60 second animated video, for parents and teachers.
- who it serves (ICP): elementary teachers and parents of kids 4 to 9 who want custom bedtime or classroom stories.
- stage: invite-only beta, 140 beta users, no revenue yet.
- goals (next 90 days): 500 beta users; 40 teachers using it weekly; pricing test.
- topics: building an AI product as a solo founder; what kids respond to in stories; teachers and classroom tech; shipping small and learning from beta users.
- tone: curious, honest, calm. Words to avoid: revolutionary, magic, disrupt, AI-powered, game-changer. <!-- allow-words -->
- proof the founder can use:
  - 140 beta users as of October 2026.
  - Average video takes 4 minutes to generate.
  - Built and run by one founder.
- off-limits: children's names or photos; claims about learning outcomes; comparisons that name competitors.
