# Writing check (use when the humanize skill is not installed)

The humanize skill (home: entrepreneur-skills) does this with real tools. If it is not
installed, run this check by hand on every draft before showing it. Say which one you used.

1. Characters. Replace em dashes with a comma, a period or "and". En dashes become hyphens.
   Curly quotes become straight. Remove zero-width and other invisible characters.
2. Words. Remove these and say it plainly instead. Also remove the active business's
   "words to avoid".

   ```
   delve, leverage, seamless, streamline, comprehensive, journey, unlock, elevate,
   empower, foster, thoughtful, relatable, humor, storytelling, simple, robust, crucial,
   utilize, synergy, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionize, tapestry, testament,
   moreover, furthermore, "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in"
   ```

3. Shapes. Rewrite "It's not X, it's Y", lists of three for rhythm, one-word questions,
   "Here's the thing", and a closing "Thoughts?" or "Agree?".
4. Rhythm. Sentences under 25 words, mixed lengths. Paragraphs of 3 sentences or fewer.
5. Facts. Every number, name and claim is either in founder-context, in what the user said,
   or written as `{{your number}}`. Flag each placeholder. Three things also count as made
   up, even when flagged: a number you worked out yourself (6 offices + 1 new = "7 on
   weekly plans"), a scene or detail you pictured ("she was watching the door"), and a
   feeling you gave the founder ("I still think about it"). Cut them or ask.
6. No emoji unless the business tone file says the founder uses them.

Report it in one line, for example: `writing check (by hand): 2 em dashes, 1 stock phrase fixed`.
